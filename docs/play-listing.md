# Paycheck Pocket — Google Play listing (draft)

**Draft in this repo only.** Do not paste this into Play Console, and do not treat it as the live store listing.

No Play Store listing file lived in `paycheck-easy`, and none turned up in the public Tun Labs site (`tunlabs-site`). This file is the review copy for the listing text.

Publisher name in this copy: **Tun Labs** only. Support email stays `support@paycheckpocket.com` and is not part of this listing.

Google Play’s short description field allows 80 characters. The short copy below is 62 characters, within that limit.

## Short description

Know your take-home before payday — no Excel, no tax guessing.

## Full description

Paycheck math shouldn’t need a spreadsheet and a guess.

Paycheck Pocket shows your estimated take-home from hours, pay, and tax settings — so you can plan rent, debt, groceries, gas, and bills with a number you can trust.

Built for people who were tired of predicting every paycheck. Open the app, see the number, and decide: tight month or a little room to breathe.

Tun Labs. Estimates only — not tax advice.
