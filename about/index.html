About Paycheck Pocket

Paycheck Pocket started with a simple question we kept hearing: "How much am I actually taking home?"

Coworkers, friends, and people around us all had the same payday routine. Some kept Excel open. Some used a calculator. Everyone was guessing how much tax would come out, and the decimals and tax rules made it stressful.

Nobody was chasing anything fancy. They just needed one clear number: what's actually coming home. With it, they could plan rent, debt, groceries, the car, gas, and bills before payday, and even decide ahead of time whether a few extra overtime hours are worth it, instead of waiting for the stub and hoping.

That number changes how payday feels. When it's low, you know to spend carefully. When it's higher, you can breathe a little. Either way, there are no surprises.

So we built the tool everyone wanted: hours in, take-home out. No spreadsheets, no tax guessing, no hype. Just clarity in your pocket, so you can stop predicting and start planning.
