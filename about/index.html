About Paycheck Pocket

Paycheck Pocket comes from a question a lot of hourly workers ask every payday: how much am I actually taking home? Too many people answer it with a spreadsheet, a calculator and a guess at the tax, then wait for the pay stub and hope.

When you're planning rent, debt, groceries, gas and bills, a guess isn't good enough. A low week means spending carefully, and a better week means you can breathe a little. You should know which one it is before payday, not after.

So Tun Labs built the clarity into your pocket: put in your hours and get your take-home number, so you can stop predicting and start planning.
