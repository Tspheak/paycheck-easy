About Paycheck Pocket

Paycheck Pocket started at our kitchen table. I was stuck in Excel every payday. My wife was on a calculator, guessing how much tax would come out. We needed a clear take-home number so we could plan rent, debt, groceries, the car, gas, and bills — not wait for the stub and hope.

Decimals and tax made guessing stressful. When the number’s low, we know to spend carefully. When it’s higher, we can breathe a little. The app is that clarity in your pocket: hours in, take-home out, so you stop predicting and start planning.
