## 4) PAYG withholding Schedule 1 (NAT 1004): VERIFIED
- Published 17 Jun 2026; applies from 1 Jul 2026 (the tax tables page says all schedules are updated from 1 Jul 2026)
- Main page: https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/payg-withholding-schedule-1-statement-of-formulas-for-calculating-amounts-to-be-withheld
- Coefficients: https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/payg-withholding-schedule-1-statement-of-formulas-for-calculating-amounts-to-be-withheld/coefficients-to-use-in-formulas-for-withholding-from-weekly-payments
- Formula: y = a·x − b, where x is weekly earnings (whole dollars plus 99c) and the coefficients are weekly.
- Fortnightly and monthly conversion: the usual Schedule 1 method is x = fortnightly/2, or monthly × 3/13 when the monthly amount ends in 33c. Withholding is then y × 2, or y × 13/3. This is UNVERIFIED because I didn't open the "Using a formula" and "Working out the weekly earnings" subpages. Check them before you build this.

## 5) Study and training loans 2026-27 (marginal system): VERIFIED
Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/study-and-training-support-loans-rates-and-repayment-thresholds (last updated 13 Oct 2026)
| Repayment income | Repayment |
|---|---|
| $0 – $69,528 | Nil |
| $69,529 – $129,717 | 15c for each $1 over $69,528 |
| $129,718 – $186,050 | $9,028 + 17c for each $1 over $129,717 |
| $186,051+ | 10% of total repayment income |
2025-26 for comparison: $67,000 / $125,000 ($8,700) / $179,286+ at 10%.
The ATO page has mistakes in its examples. Example 3 cites $179,286 (the 2025-26 figure), and Example 1 gives taxable income as both $60,470 and $60,720. The table itself is consistent: (129,717 − 69,528) × 15% = $9,028.35, which the page shows as $9,028.

## 6) Super guarantee 2026-27: 12%, VERIFIED
Source: https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/key-superannuation-rates-and-thresholds/super-guarantee (last updated 17 Apr 2026)
- 12% applies from 1 Jul 2026 to 30 Jun 2027 and onwards (Norfolk Island transitional rate: 11%)
- Payday Super: from 1 Jul 2026, SG is calculated on qualifying earnings and paid each payday
- Maximum contribution base for 2026-27: $270,830 per year (this replaces the quarterly base, which was $62,500 in 2025-26)
