Super guarantee in 2026–27

The super guarantee rate is 12% for 2026–27 and stays at 12% from 1 July 2027 (ATO).

What Payday Super changes

From 1 July 2026, employers must pay super for each payday instead of quarterly, calculated on qualifying earnings. Previously super could be paid up to 28 days after the end of each quarter.

Maximum contribution base

From 2026–27 the maximum contribution base is an annual amount: $270,830. Once your qualifying earnings for the year reach it, your employer can stop paying the minimum super guarantee for that year.

Salary sacrifice and personal contributions count towards your concessional contributions cap. Check the ATO for the current cap before contributing extra.

FAQ

Is super 12% in 2026–27?

Yes. The ATO lists the general super guarantee at 12.00% for 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027.

How often must my employer pay super now?

Under Payday Super, from 1 July 2026 super is due for each payday.

Does super reduce my take-home pay?

Not if it's paid on top of your salary, which is the usual arrangement. If your package includes super, your cash salary is the package divided by 1.12.

What if I earn more than $270,830?

Your employer doesn't have to pay super guarantee on qualifying earnings above the 2026–27 maximum contribution base of $270,830.
