FAQ

Is my pay info private?

Yes. Your pay details stay on your device, and we don't collect them. See our privacy policy for details.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep Paycheck Pocket free. Premium removes them.

My tax result looks wrong

Results are estimates based on 2026 IRS and state rules. Email us your inputs and the numbers from your pay stub (with personal info removed) and we'll take a look.

How do I restore Premium?

Open Settings › Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account you bought Premium with.
